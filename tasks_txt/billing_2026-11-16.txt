Billing Estimation & Data Quality Improvements — 2026-11-16
=============================================================

Completed Changes (zero additional user input required):

1. Due-date forecasting (`billing_service.py`):
   - Added `forecast_due_date(bill, current_due)`:
     * Calls `advance_due_date(...)` and checks historical due dates (from non-legacy
       `BillInvoice` records, or `BillPayment` fallback when < 2 invoices exist).
     * When >= 2 historical due dates all fall on weekdays (Mon-Fri), Saturday/Sunday
       forecasts are rolled forward to Monday while keeping `bill.anchor_day` unshifted.
   - Updated `refresh_invoice_forecast` to infer/maintain `anchor_day` using the median
     day-of-month across non-legacy historical invoices when >= 2 (>= 3 on update) exist,
     preventing single weekend-shifted due dates from permanently pulling `anchor_day`.
   - Updated `refresh_invoice_forecast` and `reconcile_allocations` to use
     `forecast_due_date(bill, ...)`.

2. Amount estimation (`models.py`):
   - Added `Bill._historical_charge_amounts()`:
     * Normalizes non-legacy `BillInvoice` amounts by service period length
       (`expected_days / period_days`, using the bill's median recorded period length)
       when `period_start` and `period_end` are present.
     * Deduplicates identical `(due_date, paid_date, amount_paid)` unlinked payments
       when `ledger_enabled` is False.
   - Added `Bill._wma_and_seasonal_estimate(valid_amounts)`:
     * Preserves the 4-point WMA + median outlier filter as the base estimator.
     * Blends same-season prior-year value (`0.65 * base_wma + 0.35 * yoy`) once >= 1
       full year of history exists (`monthly`: 12, `bimonthly`: 6, `quarterly`: 4) and
       the YoY value is within `[0.25x, 3.0x]` of `base_wma`.
   - Added `Bill.estimated_amount_range`:
     * Empirical MAD-based `(low, high)` range from up to 6 recent inlier records when
       >= 3 historical records exist.
   - Added `Bill.has_variable_history`:
     * True when `amount_strategy == 'fixed'` and >= 3 historical amounts have a
       coefficient of variation > 5%.

3. UI surfacing:
   - `templates/bill_history.html`: displays `(typical low–high)` next to `Next Est`
     for `rolling_avg` / `last_payment`, and `History varies (typical low–high)` when

   - `templates/daisyui_test/_bill_history_workspace.html`: displays `Typical low–high`
     or `Varies: low–high` in the estimate stat tile.
