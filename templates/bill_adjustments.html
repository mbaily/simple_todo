Opening balance, adjustments and refunds

Opening balances cover history before your first recorded event. Supplier credits and debits change the account balance without changing cash spending. Record adjustments once they take effect, using today or an earlier date. Record a refund only when money is returned to you; it reduces net spending. Enter missing invoice issue dates before recording a refund so available credit can be checked.
